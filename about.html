How We Began

A love letter to Kundan craftsmanship

"We wanted jewellery that felt like an heirloom, without waiting a generation for it."

Azéle began with a simple frustration — beautiful, traditional Kundan jewellery was either locked away for special occasions, or too delicate to actually enjoy wearing. We set out to change that: working with skilled artisans to hand-set every stone and pearl, while designing pieces light enough for a full night of dancing and sturdy enough to become a true keepsake.

Today, Azéle is two collections in one home: our Kundan Edit for weddings and festive dressing, and our Everyday Edit for the ordinary, wonderful days in between — coffee runs, brunch dates, and boardroom meetings included.

Our Craft

Handmade, stone by stone

Every Azéle piece passes through the hands of artisans trained in traditional Kundan stone-setting — a technique of encasing glass and crystal stones in gold foil, unchanged for centuries. Pearls are hand-strung, chains are hand-linked, and every finished piece is checked before it ever reaches you.
